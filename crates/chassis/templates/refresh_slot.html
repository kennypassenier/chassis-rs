{#- [ux-chassis-10] The kit's ONE refresh control, in the header row of a page. This is only the slot: chassis/refresh-control.js (loaded by every kit page and by the web-app runtime) fills it with the kp-themes auto-refresh control, so the interval, its remembered choice and the freshness line are the kit's, not the page's. A page may set `refresh_default` (seconds) for when the reader has not chosen yet; the choice, once made, is shared by every page. -#}
