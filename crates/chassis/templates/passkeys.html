Register on another device

A passkey belongs to the device that made it, so register from that device.

What happens next

Three steps on the other device.

  1. Scan the codeIt opens this dashboard on your phone.
  2. Log in once with the tokenThe phone is not signed in yet.
  3. Press Register this deviceFrom then on it signs in with its fingerprint or face.

Register this device

Your browser will ask for a fingerprint, a face or a security key.

So you can tell your devices apart in the list.
